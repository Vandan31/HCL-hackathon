Interest Subsidy Scheme on Education Loan

Abroad StudyEducation LoanHigher EducationInterest Subsidy
Details
The scheme "Interest Subsidy Scheme on Education Loan" by the Education Department, Government of Gujarat, aims to provide complete financial relief to students through 100% interest subsidy on education loans for the entire moratorium period. This includes the study period and 1 additional year after course completion.
﻿
Benefits
Interest Subsidy Assistance: 100% interest subsidy on education loans up to ₹10,00,000/- for the moratorium period (course duration + 1 year).
﻿
Eligibility
The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat.
The applicant must be a student.
The applicant must have passed HSC (Higher Secondary Certificate) with at least 60 percentile.
The applicant must have taken an education loan from a recognized bank for pursuing higher education in India or abroad.
The applicant must have a family income not exceeding ₹6,00,000/- per year.
﻿
Application Process
Online
Registration Process:
Step 1: Visit https://isel.guj.nic.in and register using your mobile number and email ID.
Step 2: Log in with OTP and change the password.
Step 3: Enter name, Aadhaar number, Ration Card number, and enrollment details correctly.
Step 4: Provide parent/guardian contact details and complete registration.
﻿
Application Process:
Step 1: Log in to https://isel.guj.nic.in and click “Apply.”
Step 2: Enter course details and fill out the online form.
Step 3: Upload all required documents in the specified format.
Step 4: Visit the designated help center within 7 days for document verification and submit the application set and collect the acknowledgement receipt. (For students abroad – parent/guardian with Power of Attorney signs and submits documents).

Ration Card.

Copy of Education Loan Agreement (Signed and Stamped by Bank Manager).
Copy of Education Loan Sanction Letter (As per KCG format, signed and stamped by Bank Manager).
Copy of Education Loan Disbursement Letter (As per KCG format, signed and stamped by Bank Manager).
Statement of Education Loan Account from first withdrawal date till current date (Signed and Stamped by Bank Manager).
Bank Interest Certificate (Signed and Stamped by Bank Manager).
Copy of First Page of Savings Account Passbook / Bank Statement / Cancelled Cheque (Any one).
Admission Letter (On College/University Letterhead with Signature and Stamp).
Fee Receipts of First Year / First Two Semesters.
Self-Declaration Certificate stating “No benefit from other scheme” (As per KCG format available on website).
Passport Copy (Only for Students Studying Abroad).
Visa or Visa Letter Copy (Only for Students Studying Abroad).
Power of Attorney (Only for Students Studying Abroad).
